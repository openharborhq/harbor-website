Harbor installs with one script on any Linux machine with Docker, but getting that machine ready is different on a mini PC at home than on a cloud server. Pick where yours will run; each guide covers that machine from start to finish.

<InstallChooser />

## Not sure which?

- **For the most privacy**, run it at home on [your own hardware](/docs/install-at-home). The disk is in your house and encrypted, and nothing about your paperwork sits with a hosting company.
- **To avoid hardware altogether**, use a cloud server: [DigitalOcean](/docs/install-digitalocean) or [Hetzner Cloud](/docs/install-hetzner) are the most direct. The data is still encrypted at rest on a separate volume, but the hosting company runs the machine it is unlocked on.
- **To look around first**, [try it on your computer](/docs/install-trial), then install for real on one of the others.

## What every install needs

Whichever you pick, the guides come back to the same few things:

- **A Linux machine of its own, with Docker.** Debian 12 is what the installer is tested on. At least 4 GB of memory; text extraction is the hungry part.
- **About 100 GB for documents, on an encrypted volume.** The database keeps the text of every document in the clear so that search is fast, so the directory holding it must be encrypted. A second disk, or a cloud provider's volume, is the simple way: the installer encrypts it for you. See [The data volume](/docs/data-volume).
- **A decision about how you will reach it**: privately through Tailscale, directly at a domain of your own, or on your own network. The installer asks. See [Reaching your vault](/docs/reaching-your-vault).
- **Somewhere for backups to go**, ideally with a different company than the machine: a Backblaze B2 bucket, an SFTP host, or a second disk. You can also decide after installing. See [Backups](/docs/backups).
- **A printer, at the end.** After setup, print the recovery information described in [The break-glass envelope](/docs/break-glass). Keep it separate from the server.

<Note label="One rule">

Do not run Harbor on the machine that is also your firewall or router. If your fanless box runs OPNsense or pfSense, the vault gets a box of its own.

</Note>

[The installer](/docs/installer) describes every question it asks, the variables for an unattended install, and what it checks.
