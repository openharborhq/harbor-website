A document arrives in the Inbox with a card: the first page, a two-sentence summary in your language, and a proposed filing. *Looks like an electricity bill from Stadtwerke München for August 2026, €84.20, due 15 September.* Below it, the category it probably belongs to, the person or thing it is about, the document's date, up to three tags, and, if the paper is asking for something, a reminder as a ticked clause you can untick: "…and remind me to pay €84.20 by 15 September."

## Accepting

You confirm with one click, or change any part first. **Review suggestions before accepting them.** Connected mailboxes can also have approved-sender rules that file automatically; see [Email](/docs/email). Accepting copies the proposal onto the document and records who accepted it and when. *Accept all suggestions* takes only the ones the model was confident about.

## What the card does on its own

- A category it proposes is always one of yours. It never invents a new one; at most it leaves a hint.
- Leaflets, newsletters and safety notices that arrive beside the bill are held back under *Probably not paperwork*: counted, one link away, deletable in bulk, never deleted silently.
- If no suggestion could be made, because you chose no model, or it refused, or its response could not be processed, the card collapses to the original and a plain form. You can enter the filing details yourself.
- If a scan failed, the card says so and offers Retry.

## After filing

The document has a page of its own: every page as it arrived, beside the summary, tags, notes, versions, and a record of who changed what. Deleting a document moves it to Recently deleted, from where it can be restored.
