Harbor runs as a small stack of containers on one Linux machine, with everything it stores on one encrypted volume. Your household reaches it over a Tailscale tailnet, your own network, or a VPN you already run; Tailscale is the easiest path, not a requirement. Paperwork gets in by upload or by email. Harbor extracts searchable text and, with a language model configured, suggests how to organize documents and track their dates. Configure nightly encrypted backups and review the monthly restore-test results in Settings.

## Start here

1. [Prerequisites](/docs/prerequisites): prepare your Linux machine, encrypted storage, and access method.
2. [Install](/docs/install): run the installer once the prerequisites are ready.
3. [First run](/docs/first-run): create your account, add a test document, and check backups.

Before adding important records, configure [Backups](/docs/backups) and [The break-glass envelope](/docs/break-glass).

Two of those containers are worth knowing by name. The **worker** extracts text and creates previews, without direct internet access. The **suggester** is the one that talks to a language model, and it sends text, never files. [The language model](/docs/language-model) says exactly what it sends, and [What runs](/docs/what-runs) describes every container.
